Crossfire Keep Privacy Policy for Android

Updated 28 October 2026

Crossfire Keep is a game. It has no accounts of its own and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or anything else about you. We, its developer, don't collect personal information from anyone who plays it. This policy says what the game keeps, where, and what Google receives when you buy, use Google Play Games or see the banner ad on the map screen.

Your progress and settings

The game keeps your progress, settings and what you own in its own files on your device.

If backup is on for your device, Android adds those files to your device's backup in your Google Account, so your progress comes back after you reinstall the game or move to a new phone or tablet. That backup is stored by Google. We can't see it, and the game's part holds only its progress and settings.

Deleting the game removes its files from your device. You can turn backup off, or delete your backups, in your device's settings and your Google Account.

Permissions

The game asks for no permissions. It uses the internet only to reach Google Play, Google Play Games and Google's banner ad.

Purchases

Purchases go through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Google Account.

To know what you own, the game asks Google Play for your purchases. Google Play answers with the products bought and when, signed by Google, and nothing about you. The game keeps only what you own, in its own files.

Google Play can ask for your password or fingerprint before every purchase, and Google Family Link lets parents approve what children buy.

Google Play Games

If you use Google Play Games, the game signs in with your Play Games profile when it starts, so your medals and Daily Siege scores are kept there. It sends Google only your progress toward each medal and your scores.

Leaderboards show your scores, with your Play Games name and picture, to other players as your Play Games profile settings allow. Google's use of this is covered by Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Without Google Play Games nothing is sent, and your medals stay in the game.

Ads

A banner ad from Google AdMob shows along the bottom of the map screen, and nowhere else: never during a level. The Supporter Pack removes it.

Every ad request, for every player, is marked as directed to children (under COPPA) and asks only for ads suitable for general audiences, without personalisation. The game leaves out the permission to use your device's advertising ID, so Google doesn't receive it.

To show the banner, count it and keep ads free from fraud, Google's software receives your IP address (from which a rough location can be told), information about your device and this app, an identifier for this app on your device, which ads were shown and tapped, and performance and crash information. Google's use of it is covered by Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

The billboards, signs, big screens and blimps inside the levels show ads built into the game. They don't send anything anywhere.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads are child-directed for everyone, and every purchase goes through Google Play, where purchase authentication and Family Link apply.

Crash reports

If you choose to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your device, Google Play sends us crash reports and statistics about how the game runs, which don't identify you. That sharing is Google's and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there's nothing for us to give you a copy of or delete. We don't sell or share personal information.

You can turn off your device's backup, and play without Google Play Games. For what Google receives, see Google's privacy policy above.

Changes to this policy

If what the game keeps or shares changes, we'll update this policy, its date and the copy in the game before the update that changes it.
