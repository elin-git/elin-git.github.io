Crossfire Keep Support

Crossfire Keep is a first-person shooter and tower-defense game for Android phones and tablets. Answers to the questions players ask most are below. For anything else, write to lin.evanslin@icloud.com.

My purchases are missing

Check that Google Play is signed in to the Google Account you bought with. Then open Settings in the game and tap Restore, next to Purchases. Everything bought with that account comes back. Stars are used up when bought, so they can't be restored, but your stars are kept with your progress.

My progress on a new phone or tablet

Your progress follows your Google Play Games profile. Sign in to Google Play Games on the new device with the same Google Account and install the game: it asks whether the progress it finds is yours. Tap It's Mine to carry on where you left off, or Start Fresh to begin at level 1 (for a family member sharing your account). A device that already has progress of its own (three or more levels cleared) keeps it.

Without Google Play Games, your progress goes with your device's backup to your Google Account. Turn backup on in your device's settings. When you set up a new device from that backup, or install the game again, your progress comes back.

My medals aren't in Google Play Games

Medals and Daily Siege scores go to Google Play Games while the game is signed in to it. Open Google Play Games, sign in, then start the game again: it sends every medal you've earned.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Google Play, so Google handles refunds: see support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637. A refunded item goes back to unlocking by playing.

Purchases by children

Google Play can ask for your password or fingerprint before every purchase (in the Play Store app: Settings, Authentication), and Google Family Link lets you approve what children buy.

Privacy

Crossfire Keep has no accounts and we don't collect personal information. Read the privacy policy.
