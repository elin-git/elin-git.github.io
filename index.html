Crossfire Keep Support

Crossfire Keep is a first-person shooter and tower-defense game for iPhone and iPad. Answers to the questions players ask most are below. For anything else, write to lin.evanslin@icloud.com.

My purchases are missing

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore, next to Purchases (there's one under a gun's buy button too). Everything bought with your Apple Account comes back. Stars are used up when bought, so they can't be restored, but your stars are kept with your progress.

My progress on a new iPhone or iPad

Your progress follows your Apple Account through iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account, with iCloud Keychain on, and install the game: it asks whether the progress it finds is yours. Tap It's Mine to carry on where you left off, or Start Fresh to begin at level 1 (for a family member sharing your Apple Account). A device that already has progress of its own (three or more levels cleared) keeps it.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds: ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com. A refunded item goes back to unlocking by playing.

Purchases by children

Every purchase goes through Apple's own payment sheet: iOS asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your password before paying, and Screen Time and Ask to Buy let you decide what children can buy.

Privacy

Crossfire Keep has no accounts and we don't collect personal information. Read the privacy policy.
