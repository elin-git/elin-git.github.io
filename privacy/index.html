Crossfire Keep Privacy Policy

Updated 12 October 2026

Crossfire Keep is a game. It has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or anything else about you. We, its developer, don't collect personal information from anyone who plays it. This policy says what the game keeps, where, and the one company it shares anything with: Google, which shows the banner ad on the map screen.

Your progress and settings

The game keeps your progress, settings and what you own in its own files on your device.

So that your progress comes back after you reinstall the game or move to a new iPhone or iPad, the game keeps a copy of those files in your iCloud. That copy is stored by Apple in your Apple Account. We can't see it, and it holds only the game's progress and settings.

Deleting the game removes its files from your device. The copy in iCloud stays with your Apple Account, so your progress can come back later.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

To check what you own even when you're offline, the game keeps the App Store's signed receipts for your purchases in your device's keychain. They name the product bought and when, nothing about you.

Every purchase goes through Apple's own payment sheet: iOS asks for Face ID, Touch ID or a password before paying, and Screen Time and Ask to Buy let parents control purchases.

Ads

A banner ad from Google AdMob shows along the bottom of the map screen, and nowhere else: never during a level. The Supporter Pack removes it.

Every ad request, for every player, is marked as directed to children (under COPPA) and asks only for ads suitable for general audiences, without personalisation. The game never asks to track you, so Google doesn't receive your device's advertising identifier.

To show the banner, count it and keep ads free from fraud, Google's software receives your IP address (from which a rough location can be told), information about your device and this app, an identifier for this app on your device, which ads were shown and tapped, and performance and crash information. Google's use of it is covered by Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

The billboards, signs, big screens and blimps inside the levels show ads built into the game. They don't send anything anywhere.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads are child-directed for everyone, and every purchase goes through Apple's payment sheet, where Screen Time and Ask to Buy apply.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers (iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements), Apple sends us crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. That sharing is Apple's and is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there's nothing for us to give you a copy of or delete. We don't sell or share personal information.

The Supporter Pack removes the banner ad. For the information Google receives, see Google's privacy policy above.

Changes to this policy

If what the game keeps or shares changes, we'll update this policy, its date and the copy in the game before the update that changes it.
