Crossfire Keep

Tower Defense Meets FPS · iPhone and iPad

Build turrets along the roads, then pick up a gun and fight beside them. Crossfire Keep is tower defense where you're on the battlefield: plan your defenses between waves, then hold the line in first person with iron sights, recoil, reloads and headshots. When a giant walks up the road, you aim for its head. When the waves pour in from every gate at once, you call down the Missile Silo.

Crossfire Keep is the first game by Evans Lin, an independent developer working alone: the tower defense game Evans wanted to play, where you fight beside your turrets. The billboards in its levels advertise the family's new bakery, Two Mice Three Cats in San Ramon, California, a small business just starting out too.

Made for three kinds of player

  • Tower defense fans24 turrets and 12 traps, each with its own job, on 90 maps whose roads cross, fork, climb ramps and jump through rifts. Each wave picks its roads: read the arrows, then build where it counts.
  • Strategy playersEnergy is tight, so every build is a choice. Mix water, ice, lightning and fire into reactions, upgrade or spread out, and on Hard the enemy adapts to what you lean on.
  • Shooter fans12 guns that handle like the real thing: sights, recoil, reloads, limited ammo and headshots. In Normal and Hard the enemy shoots back, snipers hide, and boarders storm the keep's roof.
